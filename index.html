Privacy Policy

Focus Again: App Blocker Timer · Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: Focus Again does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app keeps stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • The task you type, your chosen session length, theme and settings.
  • The name you choose for the greeting (optional).
  • How many minutes you focused each day, and per task, used for your history and the weekly reflection.
  • The session that is currently running, so the timer survives a restart.

This data is stored privately inside the app on your device. It is not backed up by the app and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Accessibility service

Focus Again uses Android's Accessibility service only to block other apps during a focus session that you start. While a session runs, it reads the package name of the app in front (for example "com.example.app") to decide whether to cover it with the countdown screen. It does not read screen content, text you type, passwords or anything else inside other apps. It does not store or transmit the app names it sees. You turn the service on yourself in Android settings and can turn it off at any time outside a session.

Do Not Disturb

If you allow "Do Not Disturb access", the app turns on Do Not Disturb at the start of each session and turns it off afterwards. It does not read your notifications.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows one notification a week summarising your focus time. It is created on your phone from the data above.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service: to block other apps during a session, as described above.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): to silence notifications during a session.
  • Notifications (optional): for the weekly reflection.
  • Run at startup: to restart the weekly reminder and resume a running session after the phone restarts.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, including us. There are no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics libraries in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
